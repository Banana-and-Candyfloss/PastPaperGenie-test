About

This is a small game site I run myself. Games here are open source, free to share, or shown with the maker's say-so. Some are embedded from the maker's own page instead of copied, inside a sandboxed frame that sends no referrer.

Saved games and your recent plays are kept in your browser. I don't get them and there's no account.

If you made something here and want it gone, or you want your game added: contact details are being set up and will be here soon.

Licence problem or takedown request: contact details are being set up and will be here soon. Saved games stay in your browser.
