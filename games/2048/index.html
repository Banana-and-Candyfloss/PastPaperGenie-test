2048

open on its own page

Slide tiles around a 4x4 board. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one with double the value. Get to 2048 to win, then keep going if you want a bigger number.

Played from the author's own page. The author says it was inspired by 1024 and Threes.

Controls
