Hextris

open on its own page

Blocks fall toward a hexagon in the middle of the screen. Turn it so the colours line up. Three or more of the same colour next to each other clear out. If the stack grows past the outline, the run is over.

Rounds are short, so it fits a five minute break.

Controls

Left and right arrow keys turn the hexagon. On a phone, tap the left or right half of the screen.
